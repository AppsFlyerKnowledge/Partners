> **At a glance**: Create a robust Partner Marketplace profile page with information about your company and its services and solutions.

## Overview

AppsFlyer Partner Marketplace is where advertisers can find new ad networks, agencies, and other platforms to work with. Create your profile and provide as much information as possible for them to see in the marketplace and make educated choices.

## General

The information in this section is dynamic and is based on your existing AppsFlyer configuration and activity:

* Partner type
* Integration capabilities
* Geography of your traffic
* Industry categories and subcategories

## Profile

Add or edit your company details (e.g., company logo (mandatory), description (mandatory), website, support languages, etc.)

We recommend filling in all the details to provide as much information as possible and make your page in the Partner Marketplace more attractive to potential customers.

## Your offering

Add details about the services you provide. We recommend adding as much information as possible for better discoverability in the Partner Marketplace.

### Overview

* **Type of services you offer:** Select from the list up to 3 types of <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/vi/docs/partner-integrations#supported-capabilities">supported capabilities</Anchor>.
* **Search tags:** Add up to 5 keywords or phrases for advertisers to find you when searching by feature. Press Enter after each keyword or phrase.
* **Offering highlights:** List the highlights of your offering as bullet points.
* **Offering description:** Provide an extensive description of your offering.

<Image align="center" alt="partner offering.png" src="https://files.readme.io/67444ccc29c930e8e4322fb999288e459b93c6189bfae9d11821f7cebdc9bb65-21120457390993.png" />

### Characteristics

Provide information about your services. Select all possible options that apply—they are used as filters when advertisers are searching the marketplace.

* **Pricing models:** Provide details about your pricing requirements.
* **Supported ad types:** Select all the ad types you support.
* **Traffic types:** Select if your traffic is Non-incentivized, incentivized, or Preload.
* **Service types:** Select if your service is Managed or Self-served.
* **Supported platforms:** Select all the platforms you support.
* **Compliance standards:** Select the relevant compliance standards to which you adhere.

### Sales

Enter the sales details about the offer:

* Link to the terms of service
* Minimum budget required for the service

### Marketing collateral

Add any additional marketing information about the offer:

* Images
* Marketing kit
* Customer success stories
* Video

## Contacts

Select either of the following ways advertisers can <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/vi/docs/partner-integrations#liên-hệ-với-đối-tác">contact you</Anchor> via the Partner Marketplace:

* **Sales contact:** Enter the contact details (email required) for the introductory email AppsFlyer sends on behalf of the advertiser. This email is sent when the advertiser clicks **Introduce me** on your marketplace details page.   
  **Note:** When no contact details are provided, the **Introduce me** button in the marketplace is grayed out.
* **Link to your landing page:** You can choose to send users directly to a landing page. When selecting this option, advertisers will see the **Contact Partner** button instead of the **Introduce me** button, and clicking it leads to your URL.  
  **Tip:** Add a UTM parameter to your link for your own tracking purposes.

## Offices

A list of your company offices.  
**Note:** This information is not displayed in the Partner Marketplace.
